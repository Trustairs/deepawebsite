The TRUST™ + AIRS® Frameworks

Directors are being asked to approve disclosures shaped by complex systems they do not govern, with data they have not seen, and logic they cannot fully explain.

Sustainability disclosures are now regulated, investor-scrutinised, and shaped by customer expectations. Simultaneously, AI is transforming how those disclosures are generated. Compliance may be ticked. Confidence may still be fragile. TRUST™ + AIRS® gives boards one view of both.

TRUST™

Five pillars

Governs what is disclosed — whether ESG data is traceable, owned, validated, and assurance-ready before the board signs off.

AIRS®

Four pillars

Governs how it is produced — whether the AI systems shaping disclosures are owned, tested, explainable, and governed across their lifecycle.
